# Power-BI-projects <br>
This repository contains all the projects related to power BI dashboards. <br>

**HR Analytical Dashboard** <br>

OBJECTIVE: <br>
This visualization project dives deep into understanding employee attrition rates and the factors influencing them within the organization. It harness HR metrics for informed decision-making.  <br>

KPI: <br>
→ Attrition rates <br>
→ Employee satisfaction scores  <br>
→ Average years at company <br>
→ Average Salary <br>
→ Average Age <br>

STEPS FOLLOWED: <br>
1. Data Extraction: Gathered relevant datasets. <br>
2. Data Transformation: Prepared the data for analysis. <br>
3. Data Loading: Imported the transformed data into Power BI. <br>
4. DAX Implementation: Employed DAX functions for calculations. <br>
5. Implementing KPIs: Developed key performance indicators for insightful analysis. <br>
6. Data Visualization: Presented findings through intuitive visualizations. <br>

KEY INSIGHTS: <br>
→ Age & Occupation: Employees aged 26-35 and those in scientific/laboratory roles show the highest attrition rates. <br>
→ Job Satisfaction & Tenure: Longer tenure correlates with higher job satisfaction and lower attrition. <br>
→ Salary & Tenure: Employees earning below average and those with 1-2 years tenure are likelier to leave. <br>
→ Attrition Frequency: Analyzed the frequency of employee departures over time. <br>
→ Demographics: Attrition also relates to marital status and gender. <br>
